# Career Guidance App

A web-based career guidance application designed to help students explore
career opportunities and make better-informed career decisions based on
their interests, skills, and assessment results.

📌 Overview

Choosing the right career can be challenging for students because there are
many different career paths, courses, entrance examinations, and
opportunities available.

The Career Guidance App aims to provide students with a centralized platform
where they can explore career options, understand different pathways, and
receive guidance through an interactive application.

🎯 Objectives

- Help students explore different career paths.
- Provide career-related information in one place.
- Help students understand their strengths and interests.
- Provide personalized career guidance.
- Make career exploration easier and more accessible.
- Provide an AI-powered assistant for career-related questions.

✨ Features

# 👤 Student Dashboard

Students can access career-related information through a centralized
dashboard.

# 📝 Career Assessment

The application can be used to assess a student's interests and preferences
and use the results as part of career guidance.

# 🧭 Career Guidance

Students can explore possible career paths and understand the skills,
education, and opportunities associated with different careers.

# 🤖 AI Career Assistant

The application includes an AI-powered chatbot that can help students with
career-related questions and provide interactive guidance.

# 📚 Career Information

Students can explore information related to different career fields,
educational pathways, skills, and opportunities.

# 🗺️ Career Roadmap

The application can provide a structured roadmap to help students understand
the possible steps required to reach a selected career goal.
